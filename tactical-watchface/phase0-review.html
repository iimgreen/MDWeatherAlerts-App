The hands still cross the sub-dials several hours a day — unavoidable on an analog dial, and visible above where the hour hand meets "STEPS". What changed is that it now reads as intended rather than as a collision.

What changed

The hands crossing the slot labels looked broken because the labels were bare text floating on the dial, with nothing for a hand to pass over. Recessing the slots into sub-dials and framing the date in an aperture seats both in the dial, so a hand crossing them now reads the way it does on a real watch.

The band went from three compartments to four to seat the heading readout. Heart rate can take any compartment you prefer — every one of them is configurable.

What changed

Two passes. First the time came down to the true centre, which cost a row — Zulu moved above it and the weather line folded into the data row. That left the two arc gauges at 9 and 3 measuring values that had moved to the bottom of the face, so the progress moved into the row, under the number it belongs to, and the row gained a recessed ground that overhangs the case.

Needs care

One watch face per app — so two faces means two listings

Verified in Google's own sample: the face lives at a fixed path, res/raw/watchface.xml, one per package, and the manifest declares no per-face service. Two faces cannot share a bundle. That means two package names, two Play listings, two sets of store assets and two review cycles — but one keystore, one shared design system, and one repo.

Available

Day of year is native

DAY_OF_YEAR is a v1 data source, and so is ISO week. The spec's fallback plan for computing DOY from month and day is not needed.

Available

Step goal needs no complication

STEP_PERCENT and BATTERY_PERCENT drive both arc gauges directly. BATTERY_IS_LOW gives the red state a real system flag instead of a hardcoded 15%.

Available

Weather has a real unavailable state

WEATHER.IS_AVAILABLE and WEATHER.IS_ERROR exist, so the phone-disconnected case can be built properly rather than guessed at.

Needs care

No second time zone source

Every WFF time source reads local time. Zulu is still buildable — UTC_TIMESTAMP is epoch-based, so UTC and any fixed offset come out of arithmetic. Its units are unconfirmed; I check that on your watch before wiring it.

Not available

No sunrise/sunset, calories, distance or floors

The health sources are steps and heart rate, full stop. Sunrise/sunset exists only as a system complication provider — it can fill a slot, but it cannot mark the outer track the way the spec imagined.

Not available

No compass, altimeter, barometer or GPS

Only the accelerometer is exposed, and it gives tilt, not heading. Nothing to fake, which is how the spec wants it.

Needs care

Google's validator still caps at WFF v4

The v5 schemas have shipped but MAX_WFF_VERSION is still 4. Targeting v5 today means building without the automated gate the spec requires on every build. This is why question 10 has a real answer rather than a preference.

Needs care

Panel resolution unconfirmed

These are drawn at 498×498 from published specs. The spec is explicit that it must be read off the hardware — first ADB command in Phase 1. Every position is a fraction of the radius, so a different panel rescales cleanly.

Night mode

You asked for Samsung's. I can get you close, but not identical — here is the gap.

Not reachable

Samsung's own night mode is not reachable

Samsung's night mode runs on exactly two faces — Simple Ultra and Ultra Analog, both first-party — and it triggers off the ambient light sensor, going red when the watch detects dim surroundings. A Watch Face Format face gets neither: there is no light-sensor data source, and Variant accepts one single mode, AMBIENT. No third-party face on any platform version can join that feature or read what triggers it.

What you get

What you get instead: the same three options, same place

Night mode ships as On / Off / Auto in the customization menu — long-press the face, Customize, swipe — which is exactly where you already change it on Samsung's faces, and it looks the same: everything dimmed, accent to deep red. On and Off behave identically to Samsung's.

The difference

Auto switches on daylight, not on room light

This is the one difference you will notice, so I would rather say it now than have you find it. Samsung's Auto goes red when you walk into a dark room. Mine goes red when the sun goes down, driven by WEATHER.IS_DAY — real daylight at your location, not a guess. Walking into a dark garage at noon will not trigger it. If weather is unavailable it falls back to a fixed evening window so Auto always does something.

What happens next

Nothing is waiting on you. Design Lock v1 is written and Phase 0 is closed.

Then Design Lock v1 gets written down and Phase 1 starts on MERIDIAN: tooling on your Mac, the emulator, building Google's official sample end to end to prove the pipeline, then the project skeleton and a minimal version of the locked design running on your actual watch.
