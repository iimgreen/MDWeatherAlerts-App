What you get

Alerts that actually arrive

Warnings, watches and advisories from the National Weather Service, checked every two minutes and pushed the moment one is issued for your county.

Radar and the seven-day

Scrubbable radar loops, hour-by-hour detail, and day-and-night forecasts for all 24 Maryland regions — 23 counties plus Baltimore City.

What your neighbours see

Flooded roads, downed trees, hail and whiteouts — reported by people nearby and confirmed by others who can see the same thing.

Rivers, air and roads

USGS river gauges, EPA AirNow readings and Maryland CHART road incidents, gathered in one place instead of five.

Community reports are not official, and their locations are shown approximately. For warnings that affect your safety, the National Weather Service is always the authority.
